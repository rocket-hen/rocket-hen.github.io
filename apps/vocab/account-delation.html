Delete Your Account & Data

Effective Date: March 19, 2025

You have full control over your account and personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data, you can do this directly inside the app.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account and all related data, please follow these steps:

  • Open the app.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Select Account.
  • Tap Delete Account.

Once confirmed, all personal data associated with your account will be permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.
