SkyPass Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the SkyPass: Part 107 Drone Exam mobile application (the "App") handles, why, and what choices you have. The App is operated by Steelkiwi Inc., trading under the commercial name RocketHen, 160 Greentree Drive, Suite 101, Dover, Delaware, USA ("RocketHen", "we").

The short version: your study progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud. We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. What Data the App Handles

  • Study data: your answers, scores, lessons, exam results, study settings and conversations with Roger, the App's AI tutor. It is stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud account. We cannot access it.
  • Questions you send to Roger: when Roger answers using our cloud service, the App sends the current exam question and its answer options, the answer you selected (if any), your message, up to six recent messages of the conversation, and related study references. Do not include personal information in your messages.
  • Purchase data: an app user identifier, the subscription you bought, and its transaction and expiry dates. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): a user identifier provided by Apple, used to link your purchases to you. The App does not request your name or email address.
  • Game Center (optional): your Mayday scores and the Game Center identity managed by Apple, used for leaderboards.
  • Technical data: a device attestation key that proves requests come from a genuine copy of the App, daily usage counters, and the IP address that any internet connection reveals.
  • Usage data: events that describe how the App is used, such as onboarding steps, lessons and tests started and finished, whether an answer was correct, its topic and the time it took, features opened, and purchases. They come with the app user identifier, a device identifier provided by Apple for our apps (IDFV), your device model, system version, country and language. They never include the text of questions, your answers or your messages to Roger. We use them only for analytics, not for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.
  • Support messages: your email address and whatever you write if you contact us.

Spoken narration is generated on your device. The App does not use the microphone. Study reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

The App does not collect your location, contacts or photos, and does not contain advertising.

2. How Roger Uses AI

Roger first tries to answer using Apple's on-device AI, which does not send your message anywhere. If on-device AI is unavailable or cannot answer, and you have agreed in the App, your request is sent through our server to OpenAI to generate a reply.

We ask OpenAI not to store these conversations for later retrieval. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train its models by default, and OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, or longer if the law requires it. Our server does not store the content of your messages.

You can decline cloud processing and keep using Roger where on-device AI is available.

3. Why We Use the Data

  • To provide the App's lessons, exams, Roger and leaderboards.
  • To process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To understand how the App is used, find problems and improve it.
  • To enforce daily limits and protect our service from abuse.
  • To answer support requests and comply with the law.

4. Service Providers

We share data only with the providers needed to run the App, each under its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI generates cloud replies from Roger.
  • Cloudflare hosts the server that relays Roger requests and keeps usage counters.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • Amplitude stores and analyses usage data.
  • Apple provides the App Store, iCloud, Game Center, Sign in with Apple, App Attest and on-device AI.

We may also disclose data when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

5. How Long We Keep Data

  • Roger conversations are kept in the App for 30 days.
  • Other study data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • Daily usage counters are deleted from our server after about 26 hours.
  • Usage data is kept in Amplitude while it is useful for improving the App, and is deleted on request.
  • Attestation keys remain on our server while they are needed to protect the service.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide subscriptions and meet legal and accounting duties.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

You can delete your account in the App: open Menu and choose Delete account. This erases your study data from the device and from your iCloud, and unlinks Sign in with Apple. A subscription is billed by Apple and is cancelled in your Apple Account settings. To have usage data or purchase records deleted too, or for anything else, email us at the address below and include your request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data sent between the App and our server is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new Effective Date.
